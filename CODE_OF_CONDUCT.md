# Code of conduct

Boost Arena is a hobby project for people who want to learn and compare their work. Keep it pleasant.

- Be civil in issues, pull requests, bot names and descriptions. No harassment, no slurs, no personal attacks.
- Play straight: one account per person, your own bots, no attempts to game the scoring. The [rules](RULES.md) say what that means in detail.
- Nothing here may be used to cheat against people in online games, and nothing that helps with that is welcome in discussions.
- Disagree about tasks, rules or methods as much as you like; keep it about the work.

The maintainers may remove comments, entries or people that go against this, and will say why. Concerns can be raised privately at yogyamehrotra@gmail.com.

This is a short version of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), whose full text applies where this one is silent.
